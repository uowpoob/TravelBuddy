# TravelBuddy 主视觉

`journey-hero.webp` 用于登录、注册页和旅行工作台横幅。使用内置 imagegen 工具生成，
1536 × 1024 像素；原始 PNG 转为 WebP 后随前端一起打包，无外部图片依赖。
画面是虚构的山湖风景，不标注为具体目的地。

生成提示：

> A wide editorial travel photograph of a tranquil turquoise lake in a lush mountainous
> valley, a small winding shoreline path, layered forest-green mountains receding into
> soft mist and distant peaks. Natural morning light, realistic textures, subtle film
> grain. Centre-right mountain and path support both tall and wide crops; darker trees
> on the left support white HTML text. Restrained forest green, jade, muted blue and
> warm ivory. No people, buildings, embedded text, watermarks, logos, UI or collage.
